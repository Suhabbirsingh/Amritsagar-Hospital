Our services

Amritsagar Hospital provides emergency, diagnostic, surgical and everyday support services in Baddi.

Emergency care

Urgent assessment and support. For emergencies, call 7832006300.

Emergency information

ECG (Cardiogram)

Electrocardiogram testing as advised by the treating clinician.

Operation Theatre

Surgical facilities for procedures planned with the clinical team.

Laboratory (Blood Tests)

Diagnostic blood tests and laboratory services.

PFT (Lung Test)

Pulmonary function testing when clinically advised.

Pharmacy

Medicine support for prescriptions issued by authorised clinicians.

Ambulance

X-ray

Diagnostic X-ray services as advised by the clinical team.

Ultrasound imaging services. Prenatal sex determination is not performed and is a punishable offence.
